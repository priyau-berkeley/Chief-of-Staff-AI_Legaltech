# Chief-of-Staff-AI_Legaltech
This is a prototype for a 'Chief of Staff app' for a legal tech firm. The "Chief of Staff AI" app sits above individual cases, providing portfolio-level intelligence, resource optimization, and strategic insights across the entire firm's operations. Value Proposition: Transform from reactive case management to predictive firm operations—make every decision backed by your firm's complete historical intelligence.​ "From Managing Cases to Managing Your Firm"—position as the evolution from case-level AI to firm-level AI, targeting managing partners, COOs, and practice group leaders.​
